# Machine-Learning-Assignment

Intervention Group Profiling (Clustering & Segment Analysis Lens)

What it is: Grouping students into behavioral clusters based on multi-variable characteristics like study time, absences, alcohol consumption (Dalc/Walc), and family support. 

ML Task & Models: Unsupervised clustering algorithms (K-Means, DBSCAN) combined with decision trees or feature importance analysis to interpret the profiles of each cluster.

Evaluation Metrics: Silhouette Score, Davies-Bouldin Index, and business interpretability of the segments.

Business Value: Helps school administrators design tailored institutional policies (e.g., targeted attendance tracking campaigns vs. study-habit workshops) mapped directly to specific student personas rather than a one-size-fits-all approach.
